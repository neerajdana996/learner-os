Grading, before an LLM is involved
Normalise whitespace, then compare against answer and accept. lo<=hi and hi >= lo are the same answer and both ship in accept. A one-token blank almost never needs a model call — which is most of why this format is cheap enough to use every day.

Two blanks is the ceiling
Three or more and a wrong answer stops being diagnostic: you cannot tell which gap the learner actually has, and the scheduler gets one boolean for a mixed result. Two related blanks — a bound and its matching step — are a single idea. Three are a quiz.
