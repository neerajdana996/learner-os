Principles
Proof, not praise
No streaks, badges or confetti. The only number that moves is one the learner earned by remembering something a day later.
Interruption is a favour
The extension takes twenty seconds and then leaves. Never a nag, never a guilt trip, always one tap to stop.
Say the real number
"67, down 4 this week" beats "Great progress!". The product is measurement; the copy should sound like it.

Fill level carries the same information as hue — full, half, empty, dashed — so the map still reads for the ~8% of men with a colour vision deficiency. Every state also carries a written label; the dot is a shorthand, never the only signal.
Green and yellow are the hardest pair to distinguish, and they are exactly the pair this product leans on.
