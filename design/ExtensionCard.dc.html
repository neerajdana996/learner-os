The twenty-second card
This appears over whatever you were already doing. It has one job and then it goes away — so it carries no navigation, no branding block, and no second call to action.

Five states, and the last two are the ones that decide whether the extension survives the week: what it does when someone keeps waving it away, and what it does when there is no network to answer into.

No progress bar, no score, no streak. Anything that isn’t the question is a reason to look away.

Snooze is a labelled button, not a clock icon in the header. An earlier version of this artboard put it beside the × as a bare glyph. A clock could mean snooze, history, or how long you have left, and the one control this product leans on people using should never need to be guessed at — the × keeps the header because dismissing is the gesture that must stay effortless.

No red, no cross, no “incorrect”. Being wrong here is the mechanism working, so the tone stays level.

The confidence tap belongs here too — an earlier version of this artboard showed it only on the correct card. Asking only when someone was right makes overconfidence impossible to measure, and how often “certain” was actually wrong is one of the numbers the pilot exists to produce. Never pre-selected, on either card.

The day in the grey box is read from the schedule, not asserted: a lapse usually returns within the hour (“later today”) and a mature card can be days out. The reassuring word is “again”.
4 · Backing off

The most important card in the set. Dismissal is information, not disobedience — and a product that ignores it gets uninstalled by day four.

No verdict, and that is not a bug. Grading happens on the server, so there is nothing to say yet about whether the answer was right — the card reports what it can honestly report, and closes.

Muted, not ochre: being offline is not a warning and not the learner’s mistake. The one thing this line must never imply is that the answer was lost. It was not — it is queued, and it will be recorded at the time it was given, not the time it syncs, because the gap is the measurement.

The chosen option stays lit and the Answer button greys out: there is nothing left to press, and offering it again would invite a second answer to a question already recorded.
