What is live
Bar
to build topic without a day number, no score, no badge. Nav hidden: leaving halfway is not a supported state.
Question
live GET /diagnostic/:id/next → the concept and one item, answer key stripped server-side.
Adaptive
live right answers push the walk forward, wrong ones fall back to a prerequisite; it stops at 15 or when the map resolves. The ticks show progress.asked.
No scheduling
live answers are recorded with surface=diagnostic and touch no card. Measuring prior knowledge must not move a schedule, or day 0 contaminates the comparison it anchors.
Confidence
live required before each answer, never pre-selected — an untapped default would silently become data.
