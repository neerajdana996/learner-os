Where each slot comes from
Score
live GET /topics/:id/map → score, 0–100. Hidden until at least one concept is taught, because a mean over nothing is 0 and a big 0 reads as failure on day one.
Trend
to build No endpoint returns last week’s score (T-077). Until it does, the caret is absent — not zero.
Topic · day
live GET /topics → title, startsAt, endsAt. Day 1 is the start date, clamped at the end.
Building
live status = generating replaces the score with the job’s own progress: progress.completed of progress.total.
Due badge
live GET /session → newConcepts.length + dueReviews.length. Absent at zero rather than showing a 0.
Initial
live GET /me → name, else the first letter of email.
In session
to build The score is withdrawn while a session is open. A number in the corner is the wrong thing to be looking at mid-recall, and watching it move is not what makes anything stick.
